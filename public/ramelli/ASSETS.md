# Ramelli portfolio screenshots

Captured from the live site at https://ramelli.chat/chat on October 12, 2026
(America/Toronto), in light and dark appearance:

- `macbook-{light,dark}.png`: current desktop workspace, 1470 × 915.
- `ipad-{light,dark}.png`: a sample conversation explaining retrieval-augmented
  generation, 1030 × 773.
- `iphone-{light,dark}.png`: a short follow-up in the same sample conversation,
  383 × 818.

These are real browser captures using the public guest experience. The sample
conversation was created for these screenshots. The phone's informational mobile
notice was dismissed using its visible Dismiss control. No account history or
private conversations are included, and no application content was composited.

The authenticated Helium integration was unavailable during capture. Images were
captured with Playwright after the shared preview returned blurry images at the
wrong viewport size. Dimensions match the portfolio's existing device frames.
